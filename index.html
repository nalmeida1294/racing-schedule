Patch notes

The latest additions and improvements to Race Control. Earlier entries follow our development milestones; date ranges group work completed across several sessions.

October 9, 2026

Standings & Table Improvements

A lightweight Indianapolis-inspired loading screen keeps the Race Control logo centered while a car advances around the track as data and images load. Reduced-motion mode uses a static progress trail.

Special Events spanning multiple days now appear on Sunday when their date range ends on Sunday. Event details retain the full date range.

F2 driver standings include starts alongside separate feature/sprint wins, poles and DNFs. Team standings now include sortable wins, poles and DNFs.

NASCAR results place Chase shields beside driver names and show stage points beside total points. Cup results and completed weekends include caution counts and race scores. NASCAR event headers now include race winners.

All three NASCAR standings tables now include sortable poles and NASCAR-reported DNF totals, with driver names frozen while scrolling sideways.

The final race in each NASCAR Chase overview spans the full row and is marked The Championship Finale.

Driver and constructor names stay visible while scrolling F1 standings, rankings and results. NASCAR, F2, F1 Academy and endurance tables also keep their driver or car/team column in view.

F1 constructor standings now sort by position, constructor, points, wins, poles, podiums and DNFs. Points is the default; tap again to reverse the order.

October 8, 2026

F1 Grand Prix & Sprint Statistics

Selected tabs align centrally across all series hubs, with the first and last tabs aligned to the edges.

F1 results and completed weekends now show race-specific VSC, safety car and red flag counts, rain scores and race scores. Results include individual driver ratings, and completed event headers show the race winner alongside the pole sitter.

Driver Rankings now group Last Race, Rating Change and Rank Change beside the season average. Season highs and lows include the event name or repeat count.

Cleaner F1 team cards place logos beside team details, with one shared head-to-head explanation at the bottom of Teams & Drivers.

Constructor standings now include GP Poles, GP Podiums and GP DNFs, credited to the team entered at each event. Both cars count individually for podiums and retirements.

Corrected DNF totals so the API's “Lapped” finish status is excluded, alongside the older “+1 Lap” format. All drivers were checked against the published Grand Prix results.

Driver standings now clearly label Grand Prix-only wins, podiums, poles, average finish, average start and DNFs. Separate sortable Sprint Wins and Sprint Points columns use published sprint results. Championship points continue to include both race formats.

October 7, 2026

Faster, Clearer Racing Reference

  • Expandable season timelines and championship snapshots remember your preference, with completed seasons and champions listed last. Snapshot portraits are centered, Academy headshots enlarged, and WEC/IMSA snapshots temporarily hidden.
  • Followed-series loading, saved-feed recovery, and nonblocking retry notices keep available pages usable.
  • Eastern Time labels and clearer feed timestamps distinguish data imports from race coverage.
  • WEC styling is included in this release.
October 6, 2026

A More Compact Homepage & More Reliable Startup

F1 race reviews now support automatic safety-car, VSC, red-flag, starter and retirement counts after races. The Admin Hub keeps just rain and race ratings editable, with race facts shown read-only.

Tighter header, race-week and timeline spacing. Championship snapshots now use equal-size cards with clearer portraits, leader gaps and gold champion styling. NASCAR timelines exclude exhibition races, and F1 Academy portraits have more room.

Independent racing feeds now load together. Stalled requests have a firm timeout, and a temporarily unavailable source no longer blocks the whole homepage. Added an in-app retry for missing data and shorter waits for unavailable images.

October 5, 2026

Special Events & Faster Results

Results screens and event-session links now check for updates automatically. Added faster event-window imports, provisional NASCAR race results before inspection, and continued checks for corrected classifications. F1 qualifying can update independently when another session source is unavailable.

Manage events in one Calendar sheet, with automatic IDs and publication. Schedules, entries, and results accept plain text on the same event row.

Added a dedicated calendar for marquee races and motorsport festivals, with multi-day dates, venue details, local session times, optional entries and results, and event photo galleries. Unverified dates appear separately under Awaiting Dates. A separate editable workbook powers the calendar.

October 2, 2026

IMSA WeatherTech & CARS Tour Hubs

CARS Tour now has one hub for Late Model Stock Cars and Pro Late Models, with a shared weekend schedule, separate dated standings, teams and drivers, results, and tracks. Includes homepage championship summaries and support for manually maintained results and timetable corrections.

  • Prepared the IMSA hub with Overview, Schedule, Standings, Teams & Drivers, Results, and Tracks, powered by a separate published IMSA workbook.
  • Added GTP, LMP2, GTD PRO, and GTD class views, individual driver and car/team championships, manufacturer standings, and session-specific driver crews.
  • Added official timing imports for practice, qualifying, and race results, class podiums, pit-stop counts, editable track media, and homepage class leaders. Historical weekends fill in during the initial syncs; future weekend session times can be entered when confirmed.
  • Kept homepage carousel tiles at the height of the tallest series so the footer stays in place when swiping.
October 1, 2026

Season Timeline

  • Homepage Series Hubs now swipe horizontally, with full-tile snapping, navigation controls and equal-height tiles sized to the tallest hub. Your selected series follow your custom order.
  • Added a Championship Snapshot below the timeline for followed series, with driver photos where available, points, WEC class leaders, and verified completed-season champions. Spoiler Mode hides championship identities.
  • Added a compact homepage timeline for followed series, with season dates, individual race progress bars and NASCAR Chase markers.
  • Announced upcoming seasons appear 30 days before their opener. Timeline rows open the series hub.
September 29, 2026

WEC Hub and Formula E Preseason Preview

  • Added the WEC hub with Overview, Schedule, Standings, Teams & Drivers, Results and Tracks, connected to official published WEC data.
  • WEC separates Hypercar and LMGT3 podiums and championships, groups driver crews by numbered car entry, and includes race, qualifying and Hyperpole results where published. Older weekends fill in as the initial import completes.
  • Added WEC homepage championship leaders, class-specific session results links, scheduled endurance race durations, and editable track details and media. Corrected overseas session times using the official schedule timestamps.
  • Prepared tabbed INDYCAR, Indy NXT and Formula 3 hubs with season-completed messages looking ahead to 2027.
  • Restored the original default series order, removed development labels from the Series Menu, and made its entries open their hub overview. Saved custom orders remain available.
  • Added a clearly labeled Formula E 2026/27 preseason preview with 21 scheduled rounds, 13 venues, 14 confirmed drivers and 8 TBA seats, verified September 29. Unannounced times remain TBA, with results and standings awaiting the season start.
  • The Formula E preview includes official source links and a verification date. It is a curated snapshot; automatic new-season data updates are not connected yet.
September 28, 2026

F1 Academy Launch and Weekend Schedule Redesign

  • Launched the F1 Academy hub with Overview, Schedule, Standings, Teams & Drivers, Results and Tracks, using shared F1 track photos, maps and details.
  • Academy includes driver and team championship leaders, sortable standings statistics, driver photos and nationalities, supporting brands, and wildcard entries. Results support qualifying, Opening Races, Reverse Grid Races and Feature Races.
  • Redesigned weekend schedules across the app with compact session cards, series-colored accents, subtle flag icons, and clearer dates and times.
  • Published results now open directly from their matching session. Pole sitters and race winners appear alongside the session, while Spoiler Mode keeps results and outcomes hidden.
  • F2 home cards now show the driver championship leader with a portrait and the team championship leader with its logo. Both cards open the relevant standings.
  • Added national flags to F2 driver cards. F2 and F1 Academy now sit directly below the NASCAR hubs in the default Series Menu order, without development labels, and open on Overview.
  • Made the Next Chase Stop schedule button compact across Cup, O'Reilly and Trucks.
  • F1 driver rating ranks are now unique, using championship position to break equal averages. Removed the Refresh Ratings button.
  • The admin driver-rating editor now shows each driver's starting position, finishing position and finish status for the selected Grand Prix or sprint.
September 25, 2026

Formula 2 Hub and Racing Hub Refinements

  • Added the Formula 2 hub with Overview, Schedule, Standings, Teams & Drivers, Results and Tracks.
  • F2 home cards show their own race winner and championship leader. Next-race cards focus on feature races; sprint races remain available in weekend schedules and results.
  • Added sortable F2 standings statistics: total wins, feature wins, sprint wins, poles, podiums and DNFs, with horizontal scrolling on mobile.
  • F2 driver cards include countries and former drivers from this season, including Nico Varrone under VAR.
  • Improved F2 weekend session imports and shared F1 track matching, including Silverstone, so existing track photos, maps and details carry across.
  • The Series Menu now opens Cup, O'Reilly and Trucks on their Overview tabs.
  • Made NASCAR next-race schedule buttons more compact and changed Biggest Movers event labels to track names.
  • Refined The Chase across all three NASCAR hubs with track and winner timelines, two gainers and two droppers, and Chase-only stage leaders.
  • Added optional chart gap labels, labeled recent Chase finishes, track photos, and matching schedule buttons.
  • F1 standings show current team logos and sortable points, wins, podiums, poles, average finish, average start and DNFs.
  • Hub navigation stays visible while scrolling schedules. Improved mobile feedback fields to prevent unwanted focus zoom.
  • Added F1 driver rating editing in the admin portal and the percentage of rated sessions with a VSC or SC in track details.

Includes the September 24 refinements. F2 statistics reflect imported results and update as historical sessions finish loading.

September 21, 2026

Redesigned home tiles, qualifying and event shortcuts

  • Event schedules link directly to available results for that event.
  • Added support for NASCAR qualifying in Cup, O'Reilly and Trucks, with lap times, speeds and separate session selection.
  • When timed qualifying is unavailable, a published starting grid can be shown instead, clearly labeled.
  • F1 and NASCAR pole sitters appear on event details, next-race overview cards and home series tiles once qualifying is available. Published grid fallbacks are labeled Starts P1.
  • Broadcast listings now include a clear Network label.
  • Redesigned home series cards with side-by-side previous and next race panels, track photos, series logos and matching accent colors.
  • The previous race shows its winner and opens full results. The next race opens its event schedule, with a pole-sitter display when available.
  • Click a championship leader to open standings, or the rest of the card to open the series hub. Spoiler Mode keeps protected results hidden.
September 19, 2026

Faster startup and driver standings

  • The home page waits for its calendars, track details, summaries and images before opening; weekend sessions and deeper hub data load separately.
  • Weekend sessions load when an event opens. Hubs request the data needed for their selected tab.
  • A brief branded loader appears when a section needs data, with retry and back options if loading fails. Cached views open without an artificial delay.
  • Driver cards show their championship position in F1 and all three NASCAR hubs.
September 18, 2026

NASCAR hubs: standings, results and the Chase

  • Cup, O'Reilly and Trucks now open on Overview with the next event, latest podium and championship leaders.
  • Added championship standings with sortable statistics, stage wins, stage points, manufacturer names and points gaps.
  • Added race and available stage results, with event and session selectors.
  • Chase grid highlights cover all three series; standings indicate calculated title elimination and schedules mark Chase rounds.
  • Added Chase Battle charts with top-five, full-grid and individual driver controls, plus win charts for teams and manufacturers in restrained brand colors.
  • Added The Chase tab with progress, contender form, movers, stage leaders and a compact schedule.
  • Added optional per-series Spoiler Mode, with protected hub views and verified pre-race standings where available.
  • Polished hub navigation and clickable cards; reserve drivers have a clearer label.
  • Organizations share one team card, with distinct full-time and part-time entry sections. Missing driver photos use silhouettes.
  • Updated home summaries, track names on schedule tiles and event selectors, manufacturer graphics and the default series order: F1, Cup, O'Reilly, Trucks.

Chase lines build from saved standings snapshots; earlier unsaved rounds are unavailable. Title elimination is a conservative calculation, not an official NASCAR designation.

NASCAR teams and race day

  • Added separate full-time and part-time team sections, with full-time drivers listed before part-time drivers within each team. An organization can appear in both sections for different car entries.

  • Removed white backgrounds behind manufacturer logos and improved their contrast on dark team cards.
  • All series schedules now jump to today's or the next race when opened. Completed seasons open at the final race, and a fixed Back to Top button stays available on schedule screens.

  • Added this dated patch history beside Send feedback.
