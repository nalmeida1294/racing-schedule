Patch notes

The latest additions and improvements to Race Control. Earlier entries follow our development milestones; date ranges group work completed across several sessions.

September 18, 2026

NASCAR hubs: standings, results and the Chase

  • Cup, O'Reilly and Trucks now open on Overview with the next event, latest podium and championship leaders.
  • Added championship standings with sortable statistics, stage wins, stage points, manufacturer names and points gaps.
  • Added race and available stage results, with event and session selectors.
  • Chase grid highlights cover all three series; standings indicate calculated title elimination and schedules mark Chase rounds.
  • Added Chase Battle charts with top-five, full-grid and individual driver controls, plus win charts for drivers, teams and manufacturers.
  • Organizations share one team card, with distinct full-time and part-time entry sections. Missing driver photos use silhouettes.
  • Updated home summaries, track names on schedule tiles and event selectors, manufacturer graphics and the default series order: F1, Cup, O'Reilly, Trucks.

Chase lines build from saved standings snapshots; earlier unsaved rounds are unavailable. Title elimination is a conservative calculation, not an official NASCAR designation.

September 17, 2026

NASCAR teams and race day

  • Added Teams & Drivers to the Cup, O’Reilly Auto Parts, and Craftsman Truck Series hubs.
  • Team cards feature team colors, team and manufacturer logos, driver photos, car number graphics, and crew chiefs.
  • Drivers are grouped by team, with full photos displayed without cropping.
  • Added separate full-time and part-time team sections, with full-time drivers listed before part-time drivers within each team. An organization can appear in both sections for different car entries.
  • Added manual team participation and Show/Hide controls, independent of each driver's classification and visibility.
  • Removed white backgrounds behind manufacturer logos and improved their contrast on dark team cards.
  • All series schedules now jump to today's or the next race when opened. Completed seasons open at the final race, and a fixed Back to Top button stays available on schedule screens.
  • Added an experimental Cup Series live race hub with running order, timing, race status, stage information, and display settings.
  • Access the Cup live hub from the event schedule, with race-day entry points on the home page and Cup overview.
  • Added this dated patch history beside Send feedback.

At this release, the Cup live hub was ready for its first live-race test; the other NASCAR live hubs, standings and results were still in development.

September 15–17, 2026

NASCAR hub foundations

  • Added Tracks tabs for the Cup, O’Reilly Auto Parts, and Craftsman Truck Series hubs.
  • Added track photos to schedule tiles, plus track maps, custom track names, locations, length, surface, banking, and year built in track details.
  • Connected Cup Series race ratings and average cautions per race, with separate counts showing how many races contribute to each average. Unrated races are excluded from the rating average.
  • Added support for historical Cup race reviews back to 2023.
  • Marked the three NASCAR hubs as in development and next to launch.
September 12–15, 2026

Race week, results, and richer track details

  • Redesigned This Racing Week with series logos, event and track names, and start times grouped by day.
  • Added compact, muted Off Week and Season Completed tiles for followed series without a race that week.
  • Expanded Formula 1 results with event and session selection for qualifying, sprint qualifying, sprints, and Grands Prix. Only sessions with available results appear.
  • Added Formula 1 teammate head-to-head comparisons for qualifying and Grand Prix results.
  • Improved Back navigation, simplified series customization, and separated upcoming series from those with schedules.
  • Added screenshot attachments to feedback, including mobile uploads and viewing in the admin hub.
  • Introduced the experimental Formula 1 live race hub with pre-race, live timing, and concluded-race views.
  • Expanded Formula 1 track details with custom names, photos, maps, flags, first Grand Prix year, and track length.
  • Polished driver rankings, podiums, championship charts, and track ratings, including a combined VSC or SC average.
September 11, 2026

Feedback and Formula 1 presentation

  • Added website feedback and a PIN-protected admin service hub for reviewing submissions and tracking site health.
  • Improved home-page tile navigation and added shortcuts from Formula 1 overview cards to standings and results.
  • Expanded driver rankings with season highs and lows, rating changes, and ranking changes.
  • Refined the championship battle chart and added driver photos.
  • Expanded season charts with driver win distribution and points by team, plus team logos and completed-race counts.
  • Added racing series logos to home-page tiles and series hubs.
September 8–10, 2026

The Formula 1 hub takes shape

  • Introduced the Formula 1 hub with overview, schedule, standings, teams and drivers, and race results.
  • Added the Series Menu and made the Race Control logo a home button.
  • Connected driver ratings and race reviews, with track rain, chaos, and race-rating averages.
  • Added season distribution charts, championship tracking, and a dedicated Tracks tab.
  • Improved the mobile home page and event navigation.
  • Added team colors and images from the spreadsheet, and refined how sprint sessions contribute to track scores.
